Cornell Marketplace

Built by three Cornell alumni, the Cornell Marketplace exists to incubate alumni, students and companies -- student-run or alumni-led -- with real buyers from the Cornell network and the open market.

CIFS Food Directory

Our Mission

It begins inside a trusted community built for action, not simulation. Companies don't pitch.
They sell. They learn. They iterate.
Real customers. Real business. Real feedback.
We are actively looking for companies and startups.

In Partnership With

How it Works

  • No subscriptions. Per-transaction fees only.
  • Cornell, Faculty and Staff carry no operational burden and no institutional liability.
  • For alumni -- a direct line to the next generation of Cornell founders. Not just to mentor, but by buying and giving real feedback.
  • Every transaction directly funds Cornell programs -- including Cornell Food Institute, Entrepreneurship at Cornell, Smith Family Institute, and Alumni Affairs.
  • SupplyBistro is launching an open data program named after Nagesh Gavirneni, MBA Operations Faculty — giving Cornell faculty and students access to sanitized, repeatable supply chain data for research and learning.
The Marketplace is designed to work alongside Cornell's existing programs, not replace them. It's the next natural step.

 When startups outgrow Cornell, nothing changes. The Marketplace is powered by SupplyBistro — the same platform, the same system. They just keep selling — into distributors, retailers, and open markets.

Clients and Startups

Abstinence Spirits

Sophisticated and full of flavor, Abstinence Spirits has non-alcoholic white and dark spirits, bittersweet aperitifs, and ready-to-pour & share spritzes expertly crafted using sustainably sourced botanicals from the diverse Cape Floral Kingdom, in Cape Town, South Africa.

Staccato Bakery

Staccato Bakery was founded by Saleem Ahmed, a Cornell MBA and former finance professional turned DJ and event producer. The name "Staccato" captures that mix of rhythm and flavor -- cookies crafted with balance, intention, and love. The bakery blends his love for music with a knack for precision and quality.

Grow The Earth

Grow The Earth is a pioneering farm to fork food company utilizing regenerative practices, automation, and AI to produce fine food products in Croydon, NH. Grow The Earth believes good health starts with data driven, clean farming. It is their privilege to feed your family.
